Wallet development at Phantasma now concentrates on Aura. Ecto and the Ecto mobile app are no longer developed. Poltergeist Lite will get one more large release, then mostly bug fixes.

- The beta runs on Android, Windows and Linux, and as a browser extension for Chrome, Brave, Edge and Firefox. iPhone, macOS and the app stores come later.
- Your keys stay on the device. The recovery phrase is encrypted with a password, and the wallet locks itself after a set time.
- Stake SOUL, claim the KCAL it generates, and spend that KCAL on fees.
- NFTs are sorted by collection, and you can send or burn them.
- dApps connect through **Phantasma Link**: in the browser through the extension, with the desktop app on the same computer, and by QR code from a phone.
- Aura is in beta, so use it with care. Download it only from **[aura.phantasma.info](https://aura.phantasma.info/)**. Nobody from the team will ever ask for your recovery phrase.
